# shield-box: map-editor box barrier (DEV shieldbox, half 4 x 10 x 10) on the duel arena: the enemy is stopped at the narrow side (d ~5, a cube of radius 10 would stop it at ~11), a unit inside is trapped.
duelunits 4 2 2
duelskip 1
duelheal 0
duelplayerbot -1
duelnobot 1
duelnorules 1
duel
waitduel 240
gameplay shield.on=1;shield.ai=1;shield.peace=0;shield.radius=10;shield.dps=20
wait 3
duelo 0 all stop
duelo 1 all stop
duelplace 0 all 100 30 90
duelplace 1 0 116 30 270
duelplace 1 1 103 30 270
wait 2
shieldbox 0 100 30 4 10 10
wait 2
shielddump t0
duelo 1 0 attack 0 0
duelo 1 1 attack 0 1
wait 4
shielddump t4
wait 6
shielddump t10
quit
