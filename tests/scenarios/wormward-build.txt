# wormward-build: build tasks for wormhole wards (BtcWormWardBuild). Loose wards near home: plain, wormhole, plain. A tagged blueprint
# refuses a plain ward (fillwrong: dropped, blueprint stays); then find/fill: Labor.Find filter (accept), the tagged blueprint turns
# into a WORM ward, the plain one into a plain ward (the delivered item decides).
newgame
waitgame 240
gameplay phantom.on=1;phantom.count=1;phantom.at=0;phantom.wardWorm=1;phantom.wardPower=0;phantom.worm=0
wait 6
wormward plain 3 3
wormward spawn -3 3
wormward plain 0 6
wait 2
wormward bp 9 -6 worm
wormward bp -9 -6 plain
wait 2
wormward fillwrong
wait 2
wormward wards afterwrong
wait 2
wormward find
wormward fill
wait 3
wormward dump end
quit
